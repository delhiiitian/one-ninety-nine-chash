The old lighthouse keeper climbed the stairs every evening. He counted each step out loud, as his father had done before him. There were one hundred and twelve steps, and he knew every crack in every one.

One night the lamp would not light. The keeper checked the wick, the oil and the glass. Out at sea, a small fishing boat was heading for the rocks. He took the old brass lantern from its hook, ran to the window and swung it from side to side until his arms ached.

The boat turned. In the morning the fishermen came to thank him, carrying a basket of fresh fish and a new wick for the lamp.
